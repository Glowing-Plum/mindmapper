    Are you sure?

    Mindmapper

    Write an outline on the left and press Generate map, or build the map directly on the canvas. Everything autosaves to this browser.

    On the canvas

    • Tab adds a child, Enter adds a sibling — both drop straight into editing.
    • F2 or a double-click edits a node; Esc cancels.
    • Arrow keys move the selection along the branches.
    • Alt with the arrows reorders, indents and outdents.
    • Drag a node onto another to re-parent it.
    • Space collapses or expands; the bubble shows how many nodes are tucked away.
    • Ctrl + B/I sets bold and italic, Ctrl + H cycles the highlight.
    • Ctrl + L, or a double-click on a line, labels that connector.

    Keeping your work

    • Ctrl + O opens a .json or .md file, and dragging a file onto the canvas does the same.
    • Ctrl + S saves back to that file; Ctrl + Shift + S saves a copy elsewhere. JSON keeps formatting, colours and line labels; Markdown keeps the text.
    • Every change autosaves, and History keeps timestamped versions you can restore.
    • Replacing a map asks first, and a restore can still be undone with Ctrl + Z.

    Getting around

    • Scroll or drag empty space to pan, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Shift + F fits the whole map on screen.
